낭독하는 법

이 원고는 정찬용 박사의 책 영어공부 절대로 하지 마라에 나오는 낭독법대로 읽는다. 아기가 모국어를 익히는 순서 그대로, 먼저 듣고 흉내 내고 소리 내 읽는다.

  1. 먼저 듣는다문단 앞 ▶를 눌러 원어민 소리를 듣는다. 뜻은 해석하지 않고 소리만 듣는다.
  2. 성대모사하듯 따라 읽는다큰 소리로 처음부터 끝까지 읽는다. 발음과 억양을 그대로 흉내 낸다. 쉼표에서 숨 쉬고, 앞에 앉은 사람에게 설명해 주듯 천천히 읽는다.
  3. 녹음해서 들어 본다읽을 때는 잘한 것 같아도, 녹음을 들으면 어색한 곳이 들린다. 그 문단만 다시 듣고 다시 읽는다. 입에 붙었다 싶으면 끝낸다.
  • 다 이해 안 돼도 괜찮다. 같은 글을 몇 번 소리 내 읽다 보면 뜻이 저절로 잡힌다.
  • 모르는 단어는 찾지 않는다. 자주 나오는 단어는 결국 뇌가 알아서 익힌다. 정 궁금한 것만 영영사전으로 보고, 한국어로 번역하지 않는다.
  • 외우지 않는다. 소리 내 읽기만 해도 머리에 남고, 필요할 때 입으로 나온다.
  • 일주일에 하루는 영어를 쉰다.
왜 이렇게 읽나

우리는 한국어를 문법책이나 단어장으로 익히지 않았다. 몇 년을 듣고 흉내 내고 말하다 보니, 어느새 머리보다 혀가 먼저 말하게 됐다.

정찬용 박사는 이걸 체화라고 부른다. 자전거나 수영처럼 몸이 익히는 거라, 한동안 쉬어도 다시 하면 된다. 외운 단어는 시간이 지나면 잊히지만, 입에 붙은 소리는 남는다.

나도 이 방법으로 영어를 익혔다. 박사님께 배우면서 처음엔 듣기만 했고, 그다음엔 원서를 소리 내 읽으며 걸어 다녔다.

방법 출처: 정찬용 박사의 영어공부 절대로 하지 마라와 그 개정판. 설명은 책 내용을 바탕으로 직접 정리했다. 원고 음성은 AI 음성이다.

내 낭독 저장

Two easy reactions

When people hear that AI is coming for jobs, most of them react in one of two ways. Some get scared and think AI will take everything. Others laugh it off, because robots were supposed to replace us twenty years ago. A builder with thirty-five years in software and hardware says both groups are half right and half wrong.

Very few jobs will disappear completely, but many tasks inside them are already moving to AI. So the real question is whether you can use AI like your own team, so that you move up one level.

The two groups that need to move first

In his view, two kinds of work are in the most danger right now. The first is any job where the computer is only used to type things in, pull things out and watch screens. The second is phone work, like taking bookings, selling, answering questions and handling paperwork. He thinks these jobs will start to be pushed out between the end of this year and 2027.

This kind of work repeats and follows clear rules, and it needs very little judgment. AI agents can already do it all day and all night, and only call a person when something looks strange.

His example is simple. Imagine you collect numbers from several teams every day. Tell an agent: every morning at nine, check the shared folders and the team chat, find what is missing, write a short summary, and tell me only when a number looks wrong. Then spend your own time on the unusual cases. If you do not, the task may go to the agent anyway, and your job may go with it.

Phone work is changing in the same way. A person at a call center might handle fifty to eighty calls a day, while voice AI can handle hundreds. The people who stay will be the ones who handle the hard cases and manage the agents.

Creative work is not safe either

Many people think creative work is safe. He says the opposite is true. Beginner and middle-level design, video, writing and coding are being taken over the fastest. Interns and juniors are most at risk, because the ladder they used to climb by learning from seniors may now look broken.

His advice is to work like a senior from day one. Let AI make the first drafts, and you decide the direction, the tone and the final quality. In movie terms, you are pushed into the director's chair without going to film school.

Where people still matter

In sales, let the agent handle the basic script, the product details and the scheduling, and spend your own time on hard customers, negotiations and relationships. In education, AI already explains things better than most of us. The teacher's job moves to something harder: helping a student see why this matters now, and getting them to actually do it.

Even factories and farms are changing. Sensors, cameras and agents already suggest when to water the fields and what to fix on a machine, and he expects robots to get much better and cheaper after 2027. The shift is the same everywhere, from the person who moves things by hand to the person who moves the system.

One step ahead

So the real change is not that jobs vanish. Work that used to need ten people can be done by one person directing ten agents, and that person spends the rest of the day on judgment, relationships and strategy. You do not need to be a perfect expert. You only need to stay one step ahead and decide that you will be good at directing AI. Start tomorrow. Pick one task you repeat every day, and hand it to an agent.
